Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

pkmnPvE is a game that runs on your own computer or phone. This policy explains what information it handles, which is very little.

What the game stores

Your saves (your Pokémon, items, money, progress and settings) are kept on your own device: in the game's save folder on a PC, and in the app's own storage on a phone. The game has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

On a PC, errors the game runs into are written to save/logs/errors.log so you can attach it to a bug report. It never leaves your computer unless you send it yourself.

When the game goes online

The game only connects to the internet for these things:

  • Playing with a friend and sending saves. When you open or join a room, or send a save by code, your device signs in to the free PeerJS connection server (peerjs.com) with a random ID so the two devices can find each other. After that, battles and saves go straight between the two devices, not through any server. Your friend sees your player name, trainer sprite and game version, and a sent save goes to the device you gave the code to. Like any internet service, the connection server and your friend's device can see your IP address.
  • Checking for updates. Options → Check for updates asks GitHub for the latest version and downloads it from there. GitHub's own privacy policy covers those requests.
  • Pictures and music. Some Pokémon sprites load from play.pokemonshowdown.com, and the lofi music streams from freetouse.com (or from the links you add yourself). These sites see an ordinary request for the picture or song, including your IP address.

Phone permissions

On Android the game only asks for the camera when you press Scan QR to receive a save. The picture is only used to read the code and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Removing your data

On a PC, saves are ordinary files in the game's save folder; delete them to remove them. On a phone, uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) removes them.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
