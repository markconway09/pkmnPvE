Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

pkmnPvE is a game that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information it handles, which is very little.

What the game stores

Your saves (your Pokémon, items, money, progress and settings) are kept in files in the game's own folder on your computer. The game has no online accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your saves or any other information anywhere - except to your own Google Drive if you choose to use cloud saves, as described below.

Cloud saves with Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive in the game's Options:

  • You sign in with Google in your web browser and approve access. The game asks only for permission to use its own app data folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata scope), plus your email address so it can show which account is connected.
  • The app data folder is hidden and private to this game: pkmnPvE cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files, and those files are never accessed.
  • When you press Export save, a copy of that player's save is uploaded to this folder in your own Drive. The last five exports are kept; older ones are deleted. When you press Import, a chosen export is downloaded back to your computer.
  • The sign-in token that lets the game reach your Drive is stored on your computer only, encrypted with your Windows account's protection, and is never included in exported saves.
  • Your saves are stored in your Google Drive account, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The developer of pkmnPvE has no access to them.

Use of Google user data

pkmnPvE's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to store and retrieve your own saves at your request. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for any other purpose.

Removing your data

  • Press Disconnect in the game's Options to revoke its access to your Google Drive. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.
  • Exported saves in the app data folder can be deleted from Google Drive's settings (Manage apps → pkmnPvE → Delete hidden app data).
  • Saves on your computer are ordinary files in the game's save folder; delete them to remove them.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
