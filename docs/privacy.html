Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

pkmnPvE is a game that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information it handles, which is very little.

What the game stores

Your saves (your Pokémon, items, money, progress and settings) are kept in files in the game's own folder on your computer. The game has no online accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your saves or any other information anywhere - except straight to your own other device when you send a save to it by room code.

Removing your data

Saves on your computer are ordinary files in the game's save folder; delete them to remove them.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
