## v0.7.1 - Horde fixes

### Horde
- **Touch joystick.** On a phone or touchscreen, put a thumb down anywhere on the field and a joystick appears there - drag to steer. A small push walks slowly and full speed comes at just over half way out, and the joystick follows your thumb if it slides past the edge.
- **The ground shows up again.** Each area's floor tiles were missing in the downloaded game and on phones, leaving a plain green field. They now show as they should.
- **Move effects are back.** The stars, sparkles, bursts, fireballs and smoke on moves and exploders now show in the downloaded game and on phones too.
