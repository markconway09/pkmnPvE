## v0.7.2 - Horde: duo synergies and the Codex

### Horde - new
- **Duo synergies.** Hold at least one move of each of two types and that pair's bonus switches on - 24 of them, like Steam Burst (Fire + Water), Thunderstorm (Flying + Electric) or Reaper (Ghost + Dark). Every pair your moves cover is on at once, and level-up cards that would complete one are a little likelier to turn up.
- **Master of Elements.** A new achievement for having all 24 duos on at least once; its title lets moves of other types set off a type's effect 70% as often instead of 50%.
- **The Codex.** The old Move list is now a full guide with tabs for moves (filter by type and shape), abilities, upgrade cards, type and duo synergies, and every item.
- **New moves.** Spikes (Ground), Stealth Rock (Rock), Mortal Spin (Poison), Ceaseless Edge (Dark), Strange Steam and Light of Ruin (Fairy). Spikes and Toxic Spikes leave hazards on the ground that hurt foes, and Toxic Spikes poison them too.
- **Ranged foes.** Wild Pokémon with more Sp. Atk than Attack now sometimes keep their distance and fire shots at you instead of walking in.
- **New held items.** Loaded Dice gives an extra reroll and a free one when no card is legendary, and the Odd Keystone calls up a ghost that fires Shadow Balls whenever you buy a chest, start a Totem Trial or fill a Tapu Ruin.
- **Pokédex marks.** The starter cards show whether the Pokémon is already in your Pokédex.

### Horde - changes
- **Range split in two.** Mega Launcher grows spread, wave, cone, blast, stream, piercing and orbit moves; Long Reach now grows melee moves and a little pickup range. Each is offered less while no move uses it.
- **Speed works in points.** Speed picks, Carbos and the Speed upgrade now add Speed points, which count for more on a slow Pokémon. Curse's slow stops at -50 Speed and then isn't offered again.
- **Multi-Attack.** Multi-shot is now Multi-Attack: shots hit one more foe, and melee, piercing and scatter moves repeat once more.
- **Level-ups give you room.** Levelling up knocks nearby foes back and keeps you safe for a second after picking a card.
- **Stronger items, rarer items.** Choice Band and Assault Vest give 10%, Expert Belt 15%, Life Orb 20%, and Carbos 4 Speed. Clear Amulet, Eject Button, Eject Pack, Relic Crown, Luck Incense, Reaper Cloth and Spell Tag are now legendary, and the last four are one per run. Never-Melt Ice now slows foes instead of freezing them, and Pearl String gives damage per 1,000 gold (up to 100%).
- **Rocks last longer.** Rock's Stealth Rock lasts 9 seconds with up to 2 spots (3 with the bonus, 6 with mastery).
- **Hurricane** now leaves a whirlwind that hurts and slows foes instead of pulling them in. **Stored Power** gains 10 power per ability instead of 15.
- **Tapu Ruins** take 30 seconds to fill and send out more waves of guardians. Max Mushrooms give up to 10 HP instead of 20, and berries no longer drop.
- **Endless mode is harder.** Foes there speed up faster (15% a minute), gain Defense as well as HP, and rise out of the ground twice as fast.
- **New move effects.** Fire moves get new flame animations, and orbiting moves can look like Stealth Rock pieces, caltrops or pink steam.

### Box
- **Evolve with Petals and Candies.** The edit window's evolve button can now spend a Friendship Petal and/or Rare Candies from your bag to reach an evolution, and shows what it will use first.

### Phone
- **Horde on phones.** The arena stays clear of the status bar, Pokémon no longer flicker when turning round, and the level-up cards and buttons fit on one screen.
