## v0.5.0 — pkmnPvE on your phone

### 📱 Android app
- **pkmnPvE now runs on Android phones.** Download `pkmnPvE-0.5.0.apk` from this release and open it on your phone to install it (Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager first). Everything is there: the box, battles, Roguelite, Draft, Max Raids and the Game Corner.
- **Laid out for a portrait screen.** The pages sit in a bar along the bottom, with the bag, Pokédex, rewards and Options at the top right, and your team in a dock just above the bar with your companion standing on it.
- **Made for fingers.** Tap a Pokémon card or a battle sprite to see its details, or hold a button to read its tooltip; tap the tooltip to close it. Holding anything with a right-click menu opens that menu.
- **Phone-sized controls.** Bets in the Game Corner and the sound volumes in Options open a large slider in a small window, and search boxes no longer pop the keyboard up on their own.
- **The back button works.** It closes whatever is open on top (a tooltip, an item's details, a window), then goes back to Home.
- **Your own background from the gallery.** Options → Background picks a picture from your phone's photos.
- Saves stay on the phone, and installing a newer APK over the old one keeps them.

### 💾 Moving your save between PC and phone
- **New: Save file in Options.** Export to file saves your progress as one `.pkmnsave` file; Import from file brings one back. Put the file in Google Drive (or send it any other way) to carry your save between your PC and your phone, either way.
- Importing replaces the current save, asks you to confirm first, and backs up the old one.
- Cloud saves exported to Google Drive on the PC are the same kind of file, so they import too.

### 🌪️ Online Chaos Draft
- **New way to play with a friend: Chaos Draft online.** In an online room, the host now picks between a normal battle and a Chaos Draft.
- In a Chaos Draft you both draft at the same time from the same tiers, each with your own packs and modifiers, then battle each other. Two more picks come every other battle.
- First to 4 wins takes the match. After 7 battles at most (ties can happen), whoever has more wins takes it.
- While you're in a room, other battles wait until you leave it.

### ✨ Smaller things
- **New game icon:** a pink Poké Ball in front of a booster pack.
- **Choosing a starter:** a brand-new save now opens the starter picker straight away.
- **Quieter greyed-out buttons:** pressing a buy button you can't afford no longer buzzes.
