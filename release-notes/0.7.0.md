## v0.7.0 - Horde mode

### Horde (new mode)
- **A new mode: Horde.** Pick one Pokémon and hold off a never-ending stream of wild ones on a big open field. Find it in the menu rail and on the Home screen, which shows your best time and KOs.
- **Pick from 10 random Pokémon.** Each lineup comes with its own area (cave, forest, ocean, the new Volcano and Desert and more) and shows each pick's HP, Attack, Defense and Speed, plus whether it can Mega Evolve. There's a 5% chance of a legendary in the lineup. The same picks stay until you clear a run or last 3 minutes, or you can pay 1,000 for 10 new ones.
- **Last 10 minutes to clear the run.** A boss turns up at 5 minutes and the final boss at 10. Beat it to go on into endless mode, with a new boss every 5 minutes - your total time is your score.
- **Clear a run to keep your Pokémon.** The Pokémon you picked joins your Box at level 1, shiny if it was shiny.
- **Moves fire on their own.** You start at level 1 with one weak move, and every move goes off on its own cooldown. You just move with WASD, the arrow keys, the mouse or touch, and click a foe to aim at it.
- **135 moves across all 18 types.** Each has a shape (shots, strikes, melee hits, streams, cones, piercing shots, blasts, scatter shots, barrages, waves and orbiting balls) and a twist of its own, like bouncing, splitting, chaining, draining, freezing or leaving damaging ground behind.
- **Level up to build your Pokémon.** Each level-up offers 3 cards: learn a new move, upgrade one you know, or take an ability (Huge Power, Sniper, Regenerator, Super Luck, Curse and more). You hold 4 moves and 4 abilities, and once full only upgrades turn up. Cards come in five rarities, and rarer ones give more.
- **Reroll, Rarity up, Banish and Move Deleter.** Reroll the cards on offer, pay gold to raise every card one rarity, banish a card so it never comes back that run, or forget a move once a run to free its slot.
- **Type effects.** Every type among your moves adds its own effect to your hits: Burn, Poison, Chill, Chain, Leech, Stealth Rock and 12 more. More moves of one type make its effect likelier and stronger, 2 unlock a bonus rule and all 4 unlock a mastery.
- **Evolution and Mega Evolution mid-run.** Your Pokémon evolves at level 20 (or 14 and 34 for three-stage lines), and every card is legendary that time. Once your Pokémon can Mega Evolve, KOs drop Mega Energy, and a full bar Mega Evolves it for 30 seconds with faster cooldowns.
- **Special Pokémon.** Ditto turns into the wild Pokémon around it, later even into elites and bosses. Minior breaks into its faster Core form below half HP, Wishiwashi schools up at level 20, and Palafin turns Hero as its last stage.
- **The horde grows.** Wild Pokémon swarm in faster and faster, an evolved elite turns up every 30 seconds, and some waves are exploders that blow up on contact. Bosses sometimes come Gigantamax with double HP, and drop Max Mushrooms when beaten.
- **Gold and chests.** Every point of experience also brings 1 gold. Spend it on the chests that keep turning up around the field (the minimap shows them) for one of 40 held items, from HP Up and Leftovers to Focus Sash, Life Orb and Rare Candy. Each chest costs more than the last.
- **Shrines.** Totem Trials summon a totem and its allies, and drop two levels' worth of experience when you beat them all. Tapu Ruins fill while you stand in them, and raise a random stat for good once full. Both also pay out gold. Two of each are on the field from the start, with more every 5 minutes.
- **Field items.** Berries heal, and Magnet, Choice Scarf, Booster Energy, Flame Orb, Icy Rock, Full Restore, Big Nugget and free chests drop around the field.
- **Lasting upgrades.** Spend money on the Horde page for upgrades every run starts with: Max HP, experience, cooldowns, Speed, pickup range, rerolls, Luck and banishes.
- **Rewards.** Each run pays money for every KO and every second you lasted, plus 1,500 for clearing it. Your best 3 runs and the last 50 are kept, with the moves, abilities, held items and damage of each.
- **Save and quit.** Pause with Esc to see your stats, upgrades and items, switch off damage numbers, hit effects, move animations, hit sounds and range rings, or save the run and carry on later.
- **Help on the page.** "How to play" explains the rules, and the move list shows every move and what its upgrades raise.

### Achievements, titles and missions
- **A new Horde achievement category.** Horde Breaker (clear a run, 10,000), Centurion (reach level 100), Endless (last 20 minutes, a random legendary), Mega Rush (Mega Evolve and last 20 minutes, a shiny Rayquaza), Shapeshifter (clear with Ditto) and Strength in Numbers (clear with Wishiwashi), plus a secret one for clearing with a shiny.
- **Three new titles.** Centurion pays 10% more money from Horde runs, Shapeshifter lets you use the Move Deleter twice a run, and Schooling starts every run with 2 extra rerolls.
- **New missions.** Get 100 KOs in a single Horde run, survive a full Horde run, and clear 15 Battle Tower floors.

### Classic
- **Two new wild areas: Volcano and Desert.** The Volcano has Fire, Rock and Dragon Pokémon, and the Desert has Ground, Ghost and Dark ones, each with its own backdrop.
- **Rayquaza Mega Evolves like in the games.** Knowing Dragon Ascent is enough, no Mega Stone needed.

### Battles
- **Hit sounds.** Moves that land make a sound matching the hit - super effective, not very effective or a critical hit. Switch them off in Options.
- **New trainer panels.** Both trainers' names, sprites and teams sit the same way, and hovering a team member's ball shows its sprite, name, HP and status.

### Battle Tower and online
- **Co-op shows your friend's look.** Your friend's title, gold name and companion now show on their battle panel.
- **Take back a choice.** Either player can undo their pick while the other is still choosing.
- **No Pokémon Center floors on no-healing difficulties.**
- **No Terastallizing in chaos drafts.**

### Phone
- **Music starts off on phones**, since it streams over your data. Options has a compact music player to switch it on.
