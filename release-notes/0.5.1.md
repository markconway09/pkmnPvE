## v0.5.1 — Send your save by code

### 💾 Saves
- **New Saves window in Options.** Every way to move or back up your save now lives behind one Saves button, split into what works on PC and phone and what's PC only.
- **New: Send to another device.** Press Send this save and a 5-letter code appears; on the other device press Receive a save and type it in. The save goes straight across, PC to phone or phone to PC, with no account or file to carry. Both need the game open and the internet.
- **Scan instead of typing.** On the PC the code also shows as a QR code, and the phone's Scan QR button reads it with the camera (Android asks for camera access the first time).
- **See it before it's replaced.** The receiving device shows the save's name, Pokémon count, money and version first, and backs up its own save before replacing it. A save from a newer version of the game asks you to update first.
- **Save file and Google Drive are still there.** Save file sits with the PC-and-phone options, and the Google Drive backup is under PC only.

### 📱 Phone
- **Doubles and Max Raids fit the screen.** The sprites are smaller and the HP boxes show just the name, level and HP bar, so nothing overlaps (the stat changes are still in the tooltip).
- **Roguelite team names fit their cards.** Long names shrink and then cut off instead of spilling out.
- **Tap a Roguelite team card to see its details**, unless you're picking a Pokémon to give something to.
- **Tooltips from a long press sit above the menu** that opened with them, and close as soon as you tap anything.
