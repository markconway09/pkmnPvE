## v0.4.8 — A secret raid boss, a daily Shiny Patch, box tools

### 🌀 Max Raids
- **A secret boss:** when a raid would bring a legendary, there's a 1% chance it's **Eternamax Eternatus** instead. It's giant with a purple glow, and once beaten it joins your box in that form for good.
- **Eternamax skips the raid HP boost:** its own HP is big enough, so it only gets Dynamax's doubling.
- **Clearer raid odds:** the Start button's tooltip now lists the chance of a Gigantamax, a red legendary, a gold legendary or the secret boss.

### ✨ Shiny Patch
- **One a day from the Coin Shop:** the Shiny Patch is no longer sold for ₽25,000 in the Shop. The Coin Shop has one a day for 5,000 coins.
- **First Draft win of the day gives one:** the Draft page shows the daily reward, and the result window says when you've earned it.
- The Shiny Patch's card in Items takes you straight to its Coin Shop prize.

### 🎰 Game Corner
- **New Coin Shop prizes:** Rare Candy for 100 coins and Exp. Candy M for 1,000 coins.
- Coin Shop goods are listed in the same order as the Items window's Recommended row.

### ⭐ Merging
- **Merge stars no longer raise HP:** each star still adds +10% to every other stat (+7.5% for red, +5% for gold).
- **No more extra growth for unevolved Pokémon:** an Everstone-locked Pokémon's stars are worth the same as anyone else's.
- **Select all in the merge window** picks every copy that can go in right now.

### 📦 Box
- **Rarity filter:** a menu to show just one rarity colour.
- **Select all when selling:** picks every Pokémon shown except favourites, shinies and your companion. A tick decides whether merged Pokémon are included.
- **Scroll buttons** down the right side: back to the top, or down a few rows.
- The Box page opens at once and shows a spinner while the grid loads.

### ⚔️ Battles
- **Item Charm reworked:** each wild drop chance goes up a flat 15 points (75% becomes 90%), instead of ×1.5.
- **Friendship Petals drop less:** the wild petal drop is down from 25% to 15%.
- **Hidden Power is one move:** sets that used a typed copy (like Hidden Power Fire) now use plain Hidden Power with IVs that give that type. The editor shows its type as you change IVs.
- The wild result window keeps one size, its buttons have icons, and "Battle again" is now **Find another**.

### 🎵 Other
- Pausing the music from the top bar now stays paused after a restart.
- The Options page is tidied into sections and shows who you're logged in as.
