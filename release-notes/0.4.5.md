## v0.4.5 — Screen size, 6-star merges & Friendship Petals

### 🖥️ Display & controls
- **New Screen size option** (Options → Screen size): **Auto** fits the game to your screen, or pick a fixed **75%–150%**.
- **Drag to scroll:** hold the left mouse button on any list and drag it, like a touch screen, with a little glide when you let go. A short click is still a click.
- The Box has an empty strip down its right side to grab and drag-scroll it.

### ⭐ Merging
- **A 6th star:** a Pokémon now reaches ★6 at **64 copies**. A ★6 Pokémon is done: it takes no more merges, is never merged away, and gets a rainbow glow.
- **Stats at 0 IVs no longer get the merge bonus.** A stat at 0 IVs is usually kept low on purpose, like a Trick Room team's Speed or a Foul Play user's Attack.
- **Online battles no longer use merge stars,** so friendly matches are fought on the sets alone.
- **Select all duplicates** can now also spend **Friendship Petals** and **Rare Candies** to evolve pre-evolutions that aren't ready yet, each with its own tick. It uses only what each one needs, and only as many as your bag holds.

### 🌸 New: Friendship Petal
- **New item, ₽3,000 in the shop:** use it from a Pokémon's right-click menu to max out its friendship.
- Your companion now shows its friendship as a small pink bar.

### ⚔️ Battles
- **New wild battle result screen:** the result and rewards on the left, and what to do next on the right: search the area, catch, battle again or head back.
- **Battle Bond works the Gen 7 way:** after Greninja knocks out a foe with a move, it becomes Ash-Greninja for the rest of the battle.
- **Z-Moves in the editor:** with Z-Move ticked, each move shows the Z-Move it becomes, and moves without one can't be picked.

### 🏆 Completing the Pokédex
- Register every species in the National Dex, and the Pokédex header and your name and title in battle turn into **gold cards**.

### 🛠️ Smaller changes
- **Roguelite:** villain nodes now say *Takeover* and show that they're free to fight (0 gems).
- **TM quick check:** when it finds something, the result shows on the ring for a moment before the prize appears.
- **Random Pokémon cases:** the result buttons sit in one big bar along the bottom of the window. Only Nice! / Done is gold.
- A Pokémon bought from the Game Corner now shows up in your box right away.
