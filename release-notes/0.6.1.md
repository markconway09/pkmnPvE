## v0.6.1 - Spanish

### Language
- **The game is now in Spanish too.** Pick English or Español from the tabs on the login screen or in Options. Every screen redraws in the new language straight away.
- **Your system language is used on first launch.** A Spanish PC or phone opens the game in Spanish; your choice is remembered after that.
- **Pokemon data in Spanish.** Over 900 moves, 560 items and 300 abilities show their official Spanish names, with the games' Spanish descriptions, along with types, natures and stats.
- **Battles, missions and rewards follow along.** Battle buttons, hints, error messages, mission text, achievement names and reward lines all use the chosen language.

### Game Corner
- **Random Pokemon cases keep the surprise.** The "new Pokemon registered" pop-up now waits until the case finishes spinning, so it no longer gives away what you're about to get.
