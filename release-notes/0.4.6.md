## v0.4.6 — Friendship Petals rework, merge stars online & long press

### 🌸 Friendship Petals
- **Daily petals in the Coin Shop:** claim **5 free** each day, and buy a pack of **10 for 10,000 coins** once a day.
- **Wild drops:** a wild Pokémon with a friendship evolution has a **25%** chance to drop a Friendship Petal, on top of its usual drop.
- Friendship Petals are **no longer sold in the Shop** for Poké Dollars.

### 🌐 Online battles
- **New "Use stars" option:** the host can turn on merge star boosts for both sides. Off by default, so a battle is fought on the sets alone.
- Merge stars now show beside a Pokémon's level in battle whenever they're raising its stats.

### 🖱️ Controls
- **Long press:** hold the mouse (or a finger) on something for half a second to open its right-click menu.

### 🧭 DexNav
- **Stop asks first:** stopping a hunt with a chain going now takes a second click, since the chain is lost.
- The Change and Stop buttons have a fresh look.

### 🔊 Sound
- **Quieter defaults:** music, cries and sound effects start at half their old volume. A volume you've already set stays as it is.

### 🛠️ Smaller changes
- A long Pokémon name in battle now shortens with "…" instead of breaking onto a second line.
