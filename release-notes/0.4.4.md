## v0.4.4 — Online battles & Chaos Draft rework

### 🌐 New: Online battles
- **Battle a friend on another computer.** One player hosts a room and shares its 5-letter code, and the other joins with it. Singles or doubles.
- Your team fights as it is in your box, merge stars and Everstone locks included.
- **Friendly only:** no exp, money or drops. Both players need the same game version.
- This replaces **Challenge a player**, which fought a saved copy of another player's team on this computer.
- The **Classic** page is now called **Battles**.

### 🎲 Draft
- **Packs now hold 4 Pokémon** instead of 3.
- **Forfeit:** a draft match or a Roguelite fight can now be given up. Run still isn't allowed there. It counts as a loss: a lost match in a draft, or the end of the run in a Roguelite.
- **Chaos modifiers reworked:**
  - Each modifier step now offers **5 at random** instead of everything, with at least one battle-start and one Pokémon modifier.
  - Half of the Pokémon modifiers come **pinned** to one Pokémon.
  - Each Pokémon can take at most **2** modifiers, so the whole team gets stronger rather than one unbeatable Pokémon.
- **Free item swap:** every modifier step lets you change one Pokémon's held item. It doesn't count as a modifier, and it replaces the old Held Item modifier.
- **More rerolls:** you can reroll once per drafting stretch and once per modifier step.
- There's now a modifier after **every** chaos battle, with two extra picks before it after the 1st, 3rd and 5th battles until your team is full.
- Hover a chaos opponent to see **their modifiers**: the battle-start effects and each Pokémon's stat boosts.

### 🧠 Smarter battle AI
- **Entry hazards:** opponents set Stealth Rock, Spikes, Toxic Spikes and Sticky Web, stop once a hazard is maxed, and clear them with Rapid Spin, Mortal Spin, Defog or Court Change when it pays off.
- They won't switch in a Pokémon the hazards would knock out unless there's no one else left.
- **Counter and Mirror Coat:** used against foes that lean on that kind of attack, and only while the user can survive the hit.
- **Destiny Bond and Perish Song:** no longer used when they'd fail or do nothing.

### 🔊 Sound
- Menu sound effects now come from one consistent pack (Kenney's Interface Sounds), and the sound picker has been removed.
- **Game Corner sounds** can be switched off on their own in Options.
