## v0.4.9 — Dice, Lock-On and a completed Pokédex

### 🎲 Game Corner
- **New game: Dice.** Set a divider anywhere from 2 to 98 on a 0–100 line and bet the roll lands over or under it. The smaller the side you pick, the bigger the payout (99 ÷ your chance to win). An arrow sweeps out to the roll and slows near the end.
- **New title: Long Shot,** from the *Against the Odds* achievement (win a Dice roll at 5% or less, plus 500 coins). A losing roll has a 10% chance to give the bet back, and a winning one a 10% chance to pay double winnings.
- **Croupier pays out more:** besides refunding a losing spin 10% of the time, a winning spin now has a 10% chance to pay double winnings.
- **High Roller stacks:** it no longer turns off the games' payout titles, so you can have both on.
- **Title badges:** each game shows badges for the titles that change it.
- Your coins now float over the game in the window, beside the tabs.

### 📖 Pokédex
- **"New Pokémon registered!" pop-ups** appear wherever you are, for each new species or new form. Click one to open the Pokédex at that entry.
- **New achievement: Pokédex Complete.** Register every species in the National Dex (forms don't count) to get the **Pokédex Diploma**. With it, the DexNav can hunt any Pokémon, forms too, registered or not. Ones never met in the wild show up only in the Lab.

### 🏆 Achievements with Pokémon gifts
- **Swords of Justice:** register Cobalion, Terrakion and Virizion to get a Resolute Keldeo.
- **Time Traveler:** own a shiny Celebi to get Dada Zarude.
- **Artificial Heart:** have a ★5 Magearna to get Original Color Magearna.
- **Golden Touch** (the Gholdengo jackpot) now also gives a Roaming Gimmighoul.
- Gifted Pokémon arrive a little under your level cap.

### 🌪️ Draft
- **New Chaos modifier: Lock-On.** The marked Pokémon's moves never miss, even against Fly, Dig or raised evasion. One-hit KO moves keep their usual odds. A second Lock-On adds +1 critical-hit stage. Opponents can get it too.
- **Chaos Intimidate hits harder:** the other side's lead now starts at -1 Attack, Sp. Atk and Speed (it was Attack only).
- **The daily Shiny Patch now takes a full gauntlet:** it's given for the first time each day you win every Draft battle, not your first single win.

### 🌀 Max Raids & battles
- Rapid Strike Urshifu and Low Key Toxtricity can now show up as Gigantamax raid bosses.
- Battle AI knows Mega Eelektross's Eelevate makes it immune to Ground moves.
