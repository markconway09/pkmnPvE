## v0.4.3 — DexNav & the Catch page

### ✨ New: DexNav
- **New key item, the DexNav.** It's a reward for the **Pokédex Scholar** achievement.
- **Hunt one species.** Pick any registered Pokémon that can appear in the wild, and the DexNav shows where it lives and the lowest level it can show up at.
- **Chains.** In a wild battle where your target can live, it appears **15%** of the time. Each one you beat or catch adds a link to the chain, and at **30 links** it appears **75%** of the time with **2× shiny odds**. Running from one or losing to one breaks the chain.
- The **Professor** title now also maxes the chain at **20** instead of 30.

### 🧭 Menu & Home
- **Catch now has its own page**, separate from Classic. The menu order is now Catch → Box → Classic → … → Game Corner.
- The Catch card on Home shows your DexNav target and chain progress.
- **Challenge a player** moved from a pop-up into the Classic page.
- A draft in progress now gets its own **Continue your draft** banner. If you have a run going too, the two banners sit side by side.

### ⚔️ Battles
- **Battle again:** after a Classic wild battle, you can jump straight into another one in the same area. If you're leaving a shiny uncaught, it asks you to confirm first.
- **New shiny animation:** rings of stars burst out when a shiny is sent out, like in the main games.
- **Smarter AI:** opponents no longer use status moves into a Substitute (Toxic, Leech Seed, stat drops…) unless the move gets through it, like sound moves or Infiltrator.
- **Fix:** merge-star boosts now apply to the stat a move actually uses. Body Press uses Defense, and Foul Play uses the target's Attack.

### 🌿 Wild Pokémon
- **More forms in the wild:** every Wormadam cloak, Pumpkaboo/Gourgeist size, Pikachu outfit, Vivillon pattern, Toxtricity, Tatsugiri, Spiky-eared Pichu, Dusk Rockruff and Roaming Gimmighoul can now appear.
- Eternal Floette counts as a mythical and never appears in the wild.
- Dusk Rockruff and similar look-alike forms now use their base form's art in every sprite style.

### ⭐ Merging & Box
- **Bigger merge growth for weak unevolved Pokémon:** at 5 stars they now reach their evolution's 2-star stats. Pichu reaches a 2-star Raichu, and Murkrow gets ×1.25 toward Honchkrow.
- **Everstone-locked Pokémon count as fully evolved,** so they keep their merge growth whatever they hold. In exchange, an Eviolite no longer boosts them.
- **Duplicates filter** now also shows the Pokémon that would be merged in.
- **New "Use evo items" toggle** for *Select all duplicates*. It also picks pre-evolutions that need an evolution item, up to the number of items in your bag.

### 💿 TMs & Moves
- **TM quick check:** the chance of finding an item instead of a TM now depends on the result. Great is **45%** and Good is **15%** (both were 30%).
- **Smeargle can learn every move** from level 1, with no TMs needed.
- **Move picker:** moves the Pokémon already knows sit at the top, and the move you're replacing is marked *Swapping*.
- **Fix:** Hidden Power types no longer clash with the plain Hidden Power entry.

### ☁️ Cloud saves
- **Google Drive sign-in** now catches it if you untick the Drive permission, and tells you to reconnect. Before, this only failed later, on your first export.
- Drive errors now explain what went wrong instead of showing a bare status code.
