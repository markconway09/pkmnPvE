## v0.4.7 — One Items window, mission & title tweaks

### 🎒 Items window
- **Bag and Shop are now one Items tab:** everything you own and everything for sale sits in one grid. Click an item to see it in a pane beside the grid, with big Use / Open / Restore buttons and the buy (×1 / ×5 / ×10) and sell options.
- **Category buttons** under the search jump the list straight to that category.
- **Friendship Petals** always show in Items, owned or not. Their card links straight to the Coin Shop's daily petals.

### 🌸 Friendship Petals
- **Petals are now a late-game item:** the Coin Shop's daily petals and the wild petal drop unlock when Max Raids open (after beating Boss Giovanni), like the Raid Crystal and Shiny Patch.

### 📋 Missions
- **"Beat a boss"** now means a **Roguelite** boss. Boss rematches no longer count.
- **New mission:** *Win a match in Draft mode* (Random Pokémon reward).
- *Beat a boss in a Roguelite run* is gone, since "Beat a boss" now covers it.

### 🏅 Titles & achievements
- **Walking Disc:** besides paying double for a TM you already own, half the time an owned TM is swapped for a new one of the same rarity.
- **Perfectionist** now needs a **purple or higher** TM search with every check a Great (it was gold only).

### ⚔️ Battles
- **Boss rematches pay nothing:** no exp, money, drops, TMs, friendship or mission progress. They're just for the fight.
- **Merge stars stand out more:** a battler's HP panel turns gold at ★5 and a slowly shifting rainbow at ★6.
- **Wild drops are never a sure thing:** every species' own drop is capped at **75%**. Some were 100% before, like Snorlax's and Munchlax's Leftovers.
